Be prepared for fire alarms in the middle of the night. Invest in a laundry basket that has a detachable bag, find comfortable shower shoes and a decent shower caddy. Every semester you have to do two BobcatChats where you just check in with your RA. Be respectful of the custodians, throw your trash in the outside dumpsters, be cautious because bugs can get into your room. Buy a comfortable mattress pad along with two sets of sheets and blankets to switch out every other week.

They do room checks after every big break like Christmas and Spring Break. During a room check they make sure you do not have anything illegal and that the room is up to safety standard. If you're bringing a car, invest in a parking permit.

Do not bring candles, an air fryer, or anything that is not either a small Keurig or microwave (700 watts). Fridges should be 2.7 cu. ft. If you want your room to smell good either get Febreze or an oil diffuser. Bring basic cleaning supplies: Clorox wipes, dust pan and broom, handheld vacuum, paper towels. Bring extra storage like a little storage cabinet or ottoman. Bring comfortable walking shoes and an umbrella — it rains a lot. Get a desk organizer. If you are lofting your bed get a bed rail because those beds are unstable.

During the first week in the dorms, try to attend the dorm events and mingle with everyone. This is how new friendships are created. Go to events in your dorm — they can be really fun and you get free stuff. Check out the SACA Instagram because they host larger events on campus. Don't be afraid to talk to your RA if you have any questions about the campus or San Marcos area.

If you plan on lofting your bed, get a bed hanger — you can hang it from your bedrail and keep your stuff with you at night. Get a sleep mask. If you want to go to sleep early and your roommate needs to do homework, this will save you so much grief. They're literally $3 at Walmart.
