Always look online for your textbooks. If you find them cheaper somewhere else, the campus bookstore will price match.

Take advantage of Boko's Lounge between classes — it's a great place to take naps if you have extra time.

Go to all housing fairs if you want free things. Apartment complexes give away free shirts, pens, mugs and more. Some hold drawings for iPads and TVs.

Take advantage of your free Amazon Prime for Students. Just remember to cancel it after six months.

If you're looking for a job, try Jobs For Cats first. Also go to Career Services on the 5th floor of the LBJ student center — they help build resumes and host mock job interviews.

Network as much as you can. Make a LinkedIn account — it's one of the most important networking tools you have. Networking is more important than grades. Who's going to get you an amazing job after you graduate? Odds are it's the person you became friends with, not your GPA alone.

Go to all events offered on campus even if you don't think they'll be interesting. Chances are, free food and new friends.

Start meeting friends right now. It's awkward and scary because you don't know anyone, but it's going to be a lot more awkward in a week when friend groups have already formed. Hang out with anyone around you. Ask people to go do things you like. Worst case you find out you won't be friends with someone and you save yourself the trouble of learning that later.
