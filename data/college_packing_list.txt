Embrace minimalism and bring a laptop and clothes. Trade or scavenge the rest.

Minimalism is the way to go. Most students never used anything except their laptop and the same 10 outfits.

Pack your essentials and basic stuff you will need in the first few weeks until you get the vibe of the town. Unless it's a really small town, you can totally buy the other stuff you may eventually need in nearby stores or order online.

Definitely bring a rug and some good blankets and pillows. Makes it feel like home and they will get used.

If you bring scissors, a hammer, tape, colored markers, or a bottle opener you'll be very popular. Many students forget basic items.

Contact your roommate about a month before move-in. See what they are willing to bring and what you both need. You don't need two microwaves but having individual mini fridges is a good idea.

Laundry detergent pods are the best for dorm living. You're already lugging your laundry bag — you don't want the extra weight of a bottle. Dryer sheets are a must.

Don't bring all your clothes if you don't plan on staying during breaks. Bring warmer weather clothes and one or two colder weather outfits to start. Switch them out when you go home for a break.

Try finding a small tool kit — scissors, tape, and a can opener.

Don't rely on yourself to actually wash dishes especially if you have communal bathrooms. Cups and tumblers are fine but plates and bowls are useless — stick with single-use ones. Do not bring metal bottles to class — it's very disruptive when dropped.

Don't bring a bunch of sentimental things. You will lose them or they will get broken. A few pictures will do.

Bring power bars. No dorm ever has enough outlets for all your things.

Don't bring your entire wardrobe. Most students wore literally 4 out of 15 pairs of shoes they brought. Just bring white, neutral, party, and dress shoes. Two pairs of slippers is sufficient.

Having a small vacuum made one student the most necessary person in their dorm.

Bring as little as possible except linens and a good mattress pad. Always overdo it when it comes to comfort and bed.

How much did students spend on average for freshman move-in? Around $200-$300 for sheets, towels, a desk lamp, fan, and hangers. Check Target and Costco — often cheaper than Amazon.
